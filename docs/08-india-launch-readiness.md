# 08 · India launch readiness

India-specific requirements depend on your entity, sector, state, customers, data and payment model. Confirm current applicability before acting.

## Foundational launch review

| Topic | Practical action |
|---|---|
| Entity and tax | Choose entity and registrations with an Indian CA / lawyer. Confirm GST treatment, invoices, foreign SaaS / RCM and state requirements for your facts. |
| Privacy | Publish a clear notice at collection points. Establish lawful processing, consent / notice flows where required, a grievance contact, retention and security measures under applicable law. |
| Messaging | For commercial communication, check TRAI sender / principal entity, header and content-template requirements with your telecom provider / BSP before sending. |
| Payments | Use an authorised payment provider for merchant collections. Complete its KYC and match the refund, settlement and customer-support flow to your product. |
| Cyber operations | Prepare incident contacts and logs. CERT-In directions and sector rules can impose obligations depending on the incident and entity. |
| Consumer terms | Use clear pricing, refund / cancellation, delivery, support and dispute terms. Sector-specific rules may apply. |

## Time-sensitive note

The DPDP Act and Rules have staged commencement provisions; do not copy old "deadline" claims into customer-facing material. Determine which provisions apply on your planned launch date, and keep a dated compliance record.
