# 10 · Budget and timeline

A useful budget separates one-time setup from monthly operations and usage costs. Keep a 15–25% contingency for unknowns and rework.

| Cost bucket | Examples | Planning question |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery & design | Research, copy, brand, wireframes, content | What must be decided before code? |
| Build | Development, integrations, QA, project management | What is the smallest release that earns learning? |
| Launch readiness | Legal review, policies, security testing, training | What could block real customers or payments? |
| Platform baseline | Domain, hosting, email, monitoring, storage | What runs even at zero customers? |
| Usage | Payment fees, SMS / WhatsApp, maps, verification, email | What is the unit cost per customer action? |
| Operations | Support, content, maintenance, backups, improvements | Who owns the service after launch? |

## 12-week illustrative sequence

| Weeks | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Offer, audience, success metric, content inventory and requirements |
| 3–4 | Wireframes, data map, technical decision and provider due diligence |
| 5–8 | Core user flow, admin operations, content and integrations in staging |
| 9–10 | Testing, security review, policy / support readiness and launch rehearsal |
| 11 | Pilot with a small controlled cohort; fix observed friction |
| 12 | Production launch with monitoring, owner roster and rollback plan |
