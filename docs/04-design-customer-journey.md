# 04 · Design a usable customer journey

Good UX is a reduction in uncertainty. On every screen, the customer should know where they are, what happens next and what will happen if something fails.

## The five-stage journey

| Stage | Design question | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Can the right customer recognise the offer in 5 seconds? | Scroll depth, source, CTA clicks |
| Evaluate | Can they verify trust, price and fit? | FAQ opens, demo starts, form exits |
| Convert | Can they complete the action without friction? | Completion rate, field errors, payment failures |
| Use | Can they reach the promised outcome quickly? | Activation, task completion, time-to-value |
| Return | Do they have a reason and reminder to come back? | Repeat rate, retention, referral |

## Accessibility baseline

- Use clear headings, visible focus states, descriptive labels and error messages that explain how to fix the issue.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate state. Keep text readable at mobile sizes and maintain sufficient contrast.
- Make forms work with keyboard navigation and give inputs explicit labels.
- Test on a modest Android phone, weak network and a first-time user — not only a designer laptop.
