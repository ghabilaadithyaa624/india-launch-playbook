# 02 · Define the offer and scope

Your website is a business system. Its job is to make a specific customer promise, then fulfil that promise reliably.

## The one-page offer test

| Question | Write your answer |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Who has the urgent problem? Be specific about role, geography and context. |
| **Pain** | What wastes time, money or confidence today? |
| **Promise** | What outcome can you credibly deliver, and by when? |
| **Proof** | Why should they trust you: case study, demo, process, guarantee, credentials? |
| **Action** | What is the one next step: buy, book, request quote, create account? |
| **Measure** | Which number proves value: qualified leads, activation, repeat use, revenue? |

*A fillable copy of this table lives in [`worksheets/offer-test.md`](../worksheets/offer-test.md).*

## Minimum viable scope

- One primary customer journey. Do not ship three audience types with three unrelated experiences.
- One payment or lead-conversion path that is tested end to end.
- One support channel with a named owner and response target.
- A clear source of truth for orders, customer data and status.
- Instrumentation for the exact action you want customers to take.
