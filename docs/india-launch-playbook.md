## 1. Executive Summary  

| Item | Key Take‑away |
|------|--------------|
| **Market opportunity** | India’s plant‑based protein market is projected to reach **₹ 12 bn by FY28** (CAGR ≈ 22 %). Urban health‑conscious consumers (≈ 30 % of the 1.4 bn population) drive demand for clean‑label, high‑protein foods. |
| **Entry model** | **Hybrid** – local co‑pack manufacturing + strategic import of specialty ingredients, sold through **Modern Trade (MT), Online Marketplaces, and a Direct‑to‑Consumer (D2C) e‑commerce hub**. |
| **Target segments** | Four psychographic clusters (A‑D) identified in the segmentation study – Premium‑Health (A), Value‑Health (B), Flex‑Convenient (C), and Price‑Sensitive (D). |
| **Positioning** | “Science‑backed, sustainably sourced, great‑tasting protein for every Indian kitchen.” |
| **First‑year financial goal** | **₹ 150 mn revenue** (≈ US$ 1.8 mn) with **gross margin ≥ 45 %** and **break‑even by Q4 FY25**. |
| **Critical success factors** | 1️⃣ Regulatory & food‑safety compliance (FSSAI, HACCP). 2️⃣ Supply‑chain resilience (local pea/oat sourcing, safety stock). 3️⃣ Brand‑building via influencer & community marketing. 4️⃣ Agile cost‑to‑acquire‑customer (CAC ≤ ₹ 300) and repeat‑purchase rate ≥ 45 %. |
| **Go‑to‑market timeline** | **Phase 1 (Pilot – Q1‑Q2 FY25)** → **Phase 2 (Scale – Q3‑Q4 FY25)** → **Phase 3 (National Expansion – FY26‑FY27)**. |

| Requirement | Action | Owner | Timing |
|-------------|--------|-------|--------|

## 4. Recommended Entry Model  

| Component | Description | Rationale |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Manufacturing** | **Local co‑packer (Tier‑2 plant, 5 k t/yr capacity)** for core SKUs; **import‑only specialty ingredients** (pea‑protein isolate, fortified micronutrients). | Low CAPEX, fast time‑to‑market, leverages existing Indian food‑grade facilities. |
| **Ownership** | 100 % brand‑owned; contract manufacturing only. | Full control over quality, IP, and pricing. |
| **Channel Mix (first 12 months)** | • **Modern Trade (MT)** – 30 % of volume (target 150 stores in metros).<br>• **Online Marketplaces** (Amazon, Flipkart, BigBasket) – 40 % of volume.<br>• **D2C e‑commerce hub** – 30 % of volume (subscription & bundle model). | Balances brand exposure, data capture, and margin. |
| **Geography** | **Phase 1:** Delhi‑NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad (core metros).<br>**Phase 2:** Tier‑2 (Pune, Ahmedabad, Kochi, Chandigarh).<br>**Phase 3:** Pan‑India (including Tier‑3). | Allows learning loop, inventory optimisation, and incremental CAPEX. |
| **Pricing Tier** | • **Premium (Segment A)** – ₹ 250 / 200 g (≈ ₹ 125 / 100 g).<br>• **Value (Segment D)** – ₹ 150 / 200 g (≈ ₹ 75 / 100 g).<br>• **Mid‑tier (B & C)** – ₹ 200 / 200 g. | Aligns with willingness‑to‑pay from segmentation; maintains ≥ 45 % gross margin. |
